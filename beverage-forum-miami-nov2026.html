The Beverage Forum — Winter Edition, Miami Beach (LatAm)

Thu Nov 12 – Fri Nov 13 2026 · Eden Roc / Nobu Hotel, 4525 Collins Ave, Miami Beach FL 33140 · produced by L.A. Libations · 58 confirmed speakers, 32 sessions, one stage · event page: events.beverageforum.com/e/2026-latin-america
Read this first — five things decide how this trip goes.
  • This is a non-alcoholic, retail-buyer conference with an alcohol minority. Go in knowing that. Of 58 speakers, roughly 14 sit in or next to our ICP (alcohol brands, importers/distributors, beer and spirits dealmakers); the other 44 are soft-drink, energy, hydration and functional founders, the retail buyers they sell to, and the organisers. The producer, L.A. Libations, is a non-alc incubator. Nobody on the programme is a US beer or spirits wholesaler. The ICP list is §3; everyone else is in §4–5 so you can place a face. [roster]
  • The one session that matters most is Thursday 2:45 p.m. — “Rewriting the Route to Market: The Future of Beverage Distribution.” Four of our best targets are on one stage for 30 minutes: Thiago Coelho (Rio Global Brands, brought Liquid Death into LatAm), Gustavo Puffelis (InBevCo, Tijuana importer/distributor), Ana Luiza Fávero (Adega Brasil, 3,000-account distributor) and Kevin Nitz (Molson Coors non-alc). Be in the front rows, and be at the 3:45 networking break that follows it. [roster]
  • No attendee list is public — but one exists, and your ticket includes it. The event page says the ticket includes “Access to the event app for direct attendee networking and meeting scheduling.” That is the Accelevents app. Log in the day you land, export or scroll the attendee directory, and book meetings through it — it is the only way to see who is actually in the room. §6 lists the 20+ people and companies who have said publicly they are going. [event page]
  • The real distributor prize is outside the hotel. Within 20–40 minutes of Eden Roc sit Southern Glazer’s (HQ, Miami), Bacardi U.S.A. (Coral Gables), Breakthru Beverage Florida (Miramar), Park Street (Brickell — 1,752 TTB label filings in 24 months, the LatAm import gateway), Eagle Brands (Miami, AB wholesaler), Gold Coast Beverage (Doral) and Shaw-Ross (Miramar). None of them is on the programme. §7 is the local target list from our own warehouse, with principals and phone numbers. Wednesday Nov 11 and Friday afternoon are the slots for those meetings. [warehouse]
  • Circana owns the data narrative in this room. Their “State of the Industry” deck is an attendee exclusive and Circana is a Pitch Slam prize partner. Do not position against them. Position beside them: what Circana cannot show a brand is the regulatory and distribution layer — who filed which label with TTB last month, which wholesaler carries what in which state, which outlets are licensed. That is the gap, and every alcohol founder on this stage lives in it. [event page, LinkedIn Oct 28]
