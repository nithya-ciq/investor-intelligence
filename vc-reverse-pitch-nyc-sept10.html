Verdict — go, but for two people and one specific reason

  1. Go. It is free, two hours, and in New York. But go for the right reason: the format has each VC state their cheque size out loud, and cheque size is precisely where five of these six firms fail for us. Two hours to hear six funds self-disqualify — or not — is cheap intelligence at any stage, and it is the only setting where you get it without six separate meetings.
  2. Only two of the six are senior enough to matter: Ashley Ryder (Partner, VamosVentures) and Katie Weiss (Principal, ffVC). The other four are an Investor, an Investor/Analyst, an Investor and a Senior Associate — sourcing-level, none able to commit capital. Ryder is the one person in the room who has personally done retail channel analytics (Estée Lauder omnichannel, Saks analytics), and ffVC is the only firm whose published cheque — $300–700k at a sub-$10M post-money — matches a round we could actually raise.
  3. Three firms are already disqualified, so do not spend the evening on them. Thomson Reuters is tax/legal/news/risk with a stated strategic-alignment requirement we cannot meet; RRE leads $5–15M financings; Insight has no pre-seed anything. But convert one of them: Angela Liu spent 2023–25 as an investor at Bain Capital Ventures — the firm where we just identified Ajay Agarwal as the right contact for vertical SaaS and supply chain. Ask her for that introduction instead of pitching her.

What this format is actually good for

Strip away the 1:1 assumption and the value is narrower but real: a 40-minute block in which six funds publicly state their thesis, target industries and cheque sizes, followed by 50 minutes of open networking. That is a fast, free way to resolve the single question that kills most of our pipeline — what do you actually write? — and to put a face to two firms whose only other route in is a warm introduction.

What it is not: a fundraising event. There are no scheduled meetings, no matching, and the four most junior attendees cannot commit capital. Anyone travelling to New York specifically for this should know that before booking.
