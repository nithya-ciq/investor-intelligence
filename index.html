Why these are misfits — the "brand-investor vs tech-investor" distinction

There are two different kinds of "BevAlc investors," and they don't overlap:

Type of investor What they fund Example portfolio
Brand investors Companies that make and sell drinks A new whiskey label · a new RTD cocktail line · a distillery · a hard seltzer brand
Tech-for-BevAlc investors Companies that build software/tools the BevAlc industry buys to operate ConsumerIQ · ALTR (alcohol-removal tech) · FourKites (logistics SaaS for distributors)

ConsumerIQ is the second kind. We don't make a drink — we make software that BevAlc companies use. The investors below only fund the first kind (brands), so even though they have "BevAlc" in their domain, they can't evaluate us. If we pitched, their questions would be wrong: "What's your shelf velocity? Distribution footprint? Case volume growth?" — none of which apply to a software company.

Pitching them wastes a meeting. But their BevAlc operator network is valuable as advisors — that's the alternative use.
