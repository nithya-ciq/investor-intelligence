Overproof — Competitive Intel & Marketing Plan

Overproof (Lymion Group Inc, DBA Overproof) · overproof.com · Miami FL HQ · ~36 employees · Founded 2018 by Marc de Kuyper (De Kuyper family, 11th generation)
What this brief is: Competitive intel on Overproof + a marketing plan for displacement. Overproof is the closest publicly-visible competitor to BevGenie AI in the BevAlc-intelligence space — but their positioning (field-execution CRM) differs materially from ours (decision intelligence for commercial teams). This brief maps where they're strong, where they're exposed, which of their customers we should target, and how to position.

Verdict — Adjacent, not head-on. Positioning contrast is the wedge.

Overproof is a field-execution CRM with a menu database + tasting-event platform, positioned as "AI-powered sales execution and business intelligence for beverage alcohol." They sell to Field Sales — the rep in the car, the account visit, the tasting-event coordinator. Their core substrate is (a) proprietary on-premise menu data (10M+ menus) and (b) brand-imported depletions and (c) rep-activity capture.

BevGenie AI is decision intelligence for supplier commercial teams — Sales Ops, Category Insights, VP Commercial. Correction from v1 of this brief: Overproof's Enterprise page DOES name Nielsen, Circana, and NABCA as data sources. The "no supplier-side data" wedge we framed initially was overstated. TTB shipment data is still not publicly claimed in their marketing — that specific integration may remain a gap — but the broader data-source-ceiling argument doesn't hold. The wedge is now about architecture and buyer (field-execution CRM vs. commercial-decision layer, sold to Field Sales vs. Sales Ops / Category Insights), not raw data-source presence.

Two structural exposures make Overproof vulnerable: (1) Zero tier-1 supplier logos publicly named — all testimonials on their homepage are sub-scale craft brands (Island Getaway Rum, Big Hat Spirits, Tequila Corrido, Crossfire Hurricane Rum) — so enterprise prospects doing diligence find no peer proof. (2) ~$4M raised total, last public round April 2020, no Series A reported — 6-year-old capital base at ~36 headcount signals either bootstrapped-and-slow or stalled-fundraise, either way a durability concern for enterprise buyers.

Best switch-target segment: mid-to-upper-mid suppliers (150K–2M cases) whose Sales Ops / Category Insights buyer is driving the tooling decision — not the Field Sales lead. Overproof sells to Field Sales; that buyer rarely controls BI budget, and doesn't own the chain-review / distributor-QBR / RGM workflows where BevGenie earns its keep. The wedge is buyer + architecture, not raw data.

Weak switch-target: early-stage craft under 50K cases. Overproof's activation service (Overproof TEAM) is genuinely useful there and our decision-intelligence framing is above their altitude.

🧩 Product modules (7 verified from their site)

Overproof Platform

CRM/planning for reps + sales reporting for managers + depletion & menu insights. Explicitly positioned for brands doing 1K–150K cases/year. This is their core mid-market SKU.

Overproof App

Field-rep mobile — route planning, account visits, follow-ups, goals. Enforces + captures rep activity.

Tastings

Off-premise sampling program management — scheduling, staffing, photos, samples poured, feedback capture. Also sold to agencies as "Tastings for Agencies."

F&B Insights / BarInsights

On-premise consumption analytics + proprietary menu database of 10M+ menus for account prospecting. This is Overproof's strongest single differentiator — data no competitor has assembled at this scale.

Sixdots

Mobile ordering in bars/restaurants (referenced in Serchen listing; less prominent on main site).

Overproof TEAM

Nationwide market-representation service launched Sep 2021. Turnkey activation staffing for brands too small to build their own field team.

Enterprise Solutions

For brands ≥150K cases/year. Includes custom AI/software dev + data syndication + "Overproof Agent" (marketed as AI-powered intelligence for enterprise tier — the "NL-query data analyst" framing in v1 was our characterization, not their verbatim marketing) + 4 "Accelerators": Sales Impact, Sales Optimization (AI lead scoring), Market Expansion (under-penetrated geo), Menu KPI Tracking.

🎯 Marketing plan — how to displace their customers

Segment A · Best switch-target (build inbound around this)

Mid-to-upper-mid suppliers (150K–2M cases) whose Sales Ops / Category Insights buyer drives BI decisions

The story: They probably outgrew Overproof Platform (which caps at 150K cases). They evaluated Enterprise but its architecture is field-execution + CRM + tastings, not the commercial-decision layer their VP Commercial actually needs. They're either currently patching with spreadsheets or evaluating VIP iDIG / Karma / Encompass — and haven't found a decision-intelligence layer above those data sources.

Positioning: "Overproof is a field-execution CRM. We're the decision-intelligence layer for your Sales Ops and Category Insights teams — the seat that owns chain reviews, distributor QBRs, and RGM decisions. Different product surface, different buyer."

Segment B · Second-best target (DOWNGRADED in v2)

Brands whose commercial buyer is Sales Ops / RGM, not Field Sales

Reframed from v1 (NABCA-gap segment): The v1 argument was "Overproof has no NABCA story." That's wrong — Overproof names NABCA on its Enterprise page. Segment B is weaker than we initially framed. What remains real: Overproof's Enterprise tier lists NABCA/Nielsen/Circana as data sources but their product architecture is still field-execution-first, not decision-intelligence-first. So the play is:

The story: Brands whose commercial function is led by a Sales Ops Director, VP Commercial, or Head of Revenue Growth Management (not a VP Sales/Field) will find Overproof's product surface awkward for their actual workflow — chain-review prep, RGM decisions, category-plan modeling. Even if Overproof has the data feeds, the interface + agent behavior is optimized for reps in the field.

Positioning: "Overproof works if your commercial function is field-first. If your Sales Ops or RGM team is running the plan, you need a product surface built for that seat. BevGenie is designed around the decision workflow, not the rep visit."

Note: TTB shipment data may still be a genuine Overproof gap (not publicly named in their marketing), but this needs demo-level verification before making it a positioning centerpiece.

Segment C · Opportunistic (agencies + activation firms)

Beverage agencies + brand-activation firms using Overproof's Tastings-for-Agencies module

The story: These agencies are doing execution work for multiple brands. Their VALUE-ADD would be if they could deliver commercial-intelligence insights to those brands, not just activation logistics. That's a natural upsell wedge for a partnership arrangement.

Positioning: "Overproof gives you the field-team logistics. We give you the story you present to your brand-clients — why their plan is or isn't working, backed by real supplier data."

Segment D · Weak target (skip)

Early-stage craft brands under 50K cases

Overproof Platform + Overproof TEAM is genuinely useful for this segment (activation staffing, first-time CRM, basic depletion tracking). Our decision-intelligence framing is above their altitude. Don't compete here.

🎙️ 60-second competitive framing (for sales calls)

"Overproof is a field-execution CRM with a menu database, a tasting-event platform, and — at the Enterprise tier — some syndicated data feeds. It's a good tool for what it does: helping small-to-mid brands manage rep activity and on-premise sampling. But if the question is 'where is depletion decaying and why,' or 'which distributor incentive should I run in Q3,' or 'what pricing move do we take at Kroger before the category review,' the product surface isn't built for that seat. BevGenie is decision intelligence for the Sales Ops, Category Insights, and VP Commercial seat — the people who own chain reviews, RGM decisions, and distributor QBRs. Different architecture. Different buyer. Both companies can name the same data sources; the difference is what the product does with them."

Overproof has a Capterra product listing (id 203678) with zero reviews and no visible G2/TrustRadius presence.
