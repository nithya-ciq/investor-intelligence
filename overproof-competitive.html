Overproof — Competitive Intel & Marketing Plan

Overproof (Lymion Group Inc, DBA Overproof) · overproof.com · Miami FL HQ · ~36 employees · Founded 2018 by Marc de Kuyper (De Kuyper family, 11th generation)
What this brief is: Competitive intel on Overproof + a marketing plan for displacement. Overproof is the closest publicly-visible competitor to BevGenie AI in the BevAlc-intelligence space — but their positioning (field-execution CRM) differs materially from ours (decision intelligence for commercial teams). This brief maps where they're strong, where they're exposed, which of their customers we should target, and how to position.

Verdict — Adjacent, not head-on. Positioning contrast is the wedge.

Overproof is a field-execution CRM with a menu database + tasting-event platform, positioned as "AI-powered sales execution and business intelligence for beverage alcohol." They sell to Field Sales — the rep in the car, the account visit, the tasting-event coordinator. Their core substrate is (a) proprietary on-premise menu data (10M+ menus) and (b) brand-imported depletions and (c) rep-activity capture.

BevGenie AI is decision intelligence for supplier commercial teams — Sales Ops, Category Insights, VP Commercial. Our substrate is TTB shipments, NABCA control-state data, and syndicated retail scan — the currencies senior commercial leaders actually price plans against. Overproof has no public claim of TTB, NABCA, or IRI/Circana scan integration. That's a real data-source ceiling and our clearest technical wedge.

Two structural exposures make Overproof vulnerable: (1) Zero tier-1 supplier logos publicly named — all testimonials on their homepage are sub-scale craft brands (Island Getaway Rum, Big Hat Spirits, Tequila Corrido, Crossfire Hurricane Rum) — so enterprise prospects doing diligence find no peer proof. (2) ~$4M raised total, last public round April 2020, no Series A reported — 6-year-old capital base at ~36 headcount signals either bootstrapped-and-slow or stalled-fundraise, either way a durability concern for enterprise buyers.

Best switch-target segment: mid-to-upper-mid suppliers (150K–2M cases) that outgrew Overproof Platform, hit its data-source ceiling on Enterprise, and whose Sales Ops / Category Insights buyer is driving the tooling decision (not the Field Sales lead — Overproof sells to Field Sales, and that buyer rarely controls BI budget). Control-state-heavy brands are also a strong target given Overproof has no NABCA story.

Weak switch-target: early-stage craft under 50K cases. Overproof's activation service (Overproof TEAM) is genuinely useful there and our decision-intelligence framing is above their altitude.

🧩 Product modules (7 verified from their site)

Overproof Platform

CRM/planning for reps + sales reporting for managers + depletion & menu insights. Explicitly positioned for brands doing 1K–150K cases/year. This is their core mid-market SKU.

Overproof App

Field-rep mobile — route planning, account visits, follow-ups, goals. Enforces + captures rep activity.

Tastings

Off-premise sampling program management — scheduling, staffing, photos, samples poured, feedback capture. Also sold to agencies as "Tastings for Agencies."

F&B Insights / BarInsights

On-premise consumption analytics + proprietary menu database of 10M+ menus for account prospecting. This is Overproof's strongest single differentiator — data no competitor has assembled at this scale.

Enterprise Solutions

For brands ≥150K cases/year. Includes custom AI/software dev + data syndication + "Overproof Agent" (NL-query data analyst) + 4 "Accelerators": Sales Impact, Sales Optimization (AI lead scoring), Market Expansion (under-penetrated geo), Menu KPI Tracking.

Data sources — the critical gap

⚠️ No public claim of TTB, NABCA, or IRI/Circana scan integration

Overproof's data spine appears to be (1) proprietary on-premise menu data, (2) depletion feeds imported per brand, (3) tasting-event capture, (4) field-rep activity. That's a real substrate for field execution, but it is NOT the substrate senior commercial leaders use to plan. TTB shipments + NABCA control-state + syndicated retail scan are the currencies of chain reviews, distributor QBRs, and pricing decisions. Overproof has no public story here.

This is our clearest technical wedge. "We're built on the data your VP Commercial is already using; Overproof isn't."

🎯 Marketing plan — how to displace their customers

Segment A · Best switch-target (build inbound around this)

Mid-to-upper-mid suppliers (150K–2M cases) whose Sales Ops / Category Insights buyer drives BI decisions

The story: They probably outgrew Overproof Platform (which caps at 150K cases). They evaluated Enterprise but the data-source ceiling (no TTB / no NABCA / no scan) killed it for their VP Commercial. They're either currently patching with spreadsheets or evaluating VIP iDIG / Karma / Encompass — and haven't found a decision-intelligence layer above those data sources.

Positioning: "Overproof is a field-execution CRM. We're the decision-intelligence layer on top of your existing distributor data — TTB, NABCA, syndicated scan — built for the people who actually own the plan, not the reps who execute it."

Segment B · Second-best target

Brands with heavy control-state exposure (NABCA states)

The story: NABCA control-state markets (17 states + Montgomery County + parts of NC) drive a huge chunk of spirits revenue. Overproof has no visible NABCA story. Any brand with meaningful control-state exposure has to solve that data problem manually today.

Positioning: "If you're doing chain reviews in Pennsylvania, Virginia, Michigan, Ohio, or Utah, you need NABCA depletion + pricing intelligence at your fingertips. Overproof doesn't have that. We do."

Segment C · Opportunistic (agencies + activation firms)

Beverage agencies + brand-activation firms using Overproof's Tastings-for-Agencies module

The story: These agencies are doing execution work for multiple brands. Their VALUE-ADD would be if they could deliver commercial-intelligence insights to those brands, not just activation logistics. That's a natural upsell wedge for a partnership arrangement.

Positioning: "Overproof gives you the field-team logistics. We give you the story you present to your brand-clients — why their plan is or isn't working, backed by real supplier data."

Segment D · Weak target (skip)

Early-stage craft brands under 50K cases

Overproof Platform + Overproof TEAM is genuinely useful for this segment (activation staffing, first-time CRM, basic depletion tracking). Our decision-intelligence framing is above their altitude. Don't compete here.

🎙️ 60-second competitive framing (for sales calls)

"Overproof is a field-execution CRM with a menu database and a tasting-event platform. It's a good tool for what it does — helping small brands manage rep activity and on-premise sampling. But if your question is 'where is depletion decaying and why,' or 'which distributor incentive should I run in Q3,' or 'what pricing move do we take at Kroger before the category review,' Overproof isn't built for that. BevGenie is decision intelligence for your commercial team — Sales Ops, Category Insights, VP Commercial — built on TTB, NABCA, and syndicated scan. Different substrate. Different buyer."

📣 Channel + tactics for the marketing push

  • LinkedIn targeting: job-title layer (VP Sales, VP Commercial, Sales Ops Director, Category Insights Manager, Head of Revenue Growth Management) at supplier brands in the 150K–2M case range. Overproof's targeting is Field Sales; we go one seat up.
  • Content angle: "The data your commercial team needs isn't in your CRM" — thought-leadership content that names the TTB/NABCA/scan gap without directly attacking Overproof. Position implicitly.
  • Event presence: BevNET Live NYC/LA, Wine & Spirits Wholesalers Convention, and NABCA annual conference — where the Sales Ops + Category Insights buyer actually is. Overproof shows up more at brand-founder events.
  • Case-study manufacturing: First 2-3 BevGenie customer wins should be documented publicly with named logos + specific outcome numbers — direct contrast to Overproof's "small-craft-testimonial-only" pattern.
  • Analyst positioning: Get on G2 / Capterra / TrustRadius early. Overproof has zero populated review pages. First-mover advantage on "AI for BevAlc supplier commercial teams" as a review category.
  • Partnership plays: Warm relationships with VIP (iDIG/KARMA), SPINS, Circana. If we can position as the decision layer ABOVE their data feeds, we become their upsell path — and become an incumbent-side threat to Overproof's own iDIG-substitute play.
