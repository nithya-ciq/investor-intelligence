🎯 The white space — our strongest structural argument

Nobody in this set sells syndicated depletions/TTB/NABCA analytics

Across four parallel sweeps covering VIP, Encompass, Leadbeam, Overproof, Glimpse, Provi/SevenFifty, Park Street, Fintech/Lilypad, Andavi/GreatVines, GoSpotCheck/FORM, Repsly, Thirstie, Scotch and Santé — no player was confirmed to sell a syndicated three-tier market-data analytics product.

The incumbents own transactional data — orders, routes, invoices, scans — and are racing agentic AI on top of it. Only VIP (iDIG) and Encompass (OrderIQ/depletion velocity) touch depletions at all, and neither surfaced a syndicated market-data surface.

This is the gap our architecture argument should sit on.

⚠️ But the TTB data story is NOT a "the environment is changing" argument

Checked directly at ttb.gov: no 2026 change to COLA data availability. Not found The Public COLA Registry is live, free, no registration required, page last updated Jan 2, 2026. No outage, deprecation, access restriction, rate limiting or API change. NABCA: no 2026 licensing or access change found (caveat: NABCA terms are commercial and not public — absence of public evidence ≠ absence of change; a direct inquiry would be needed).

So there is no 2026 regulatory catalyst here. If our narrative needs "the data environment is shifting, creating an opening," this does not support it.

The durable argument is the opposite, and it's stronger: TTB data is stably public but structurally hard to use — HTML/XML/CSV extracts, no modern REST API, ~48-hour lag, pre-1999 gaps — and NABCA is stably expensive and licensed.
